Background Removal — Private, Fast, and Precise

Make product photos, profile pictures, and thumbnails pop. This tool erases backgrounds on your device, then exports a clean transparent PNG. No uploads, no watermarks, and no tracking.

  • Two modes: Color Pick (auto-erase similar colors) and Lasso (manual selection).
  • Edge control: Tolerance & Feather for smooth, natural cutouts.
  • Device-friendly: Works on desktop and mobile; just load and go.

How to Use

  1. Upload an image (PNG/JPG/WebP/HEIC…)
  2. Choose Color Picker then click background areas, or switch to Lasso to draw around your subject.
  3. Adjust Tolerance (more = broader color match) and Feather (softens edges).
  4. Toggle Invert to keep only selected colors for creative effects.
  5. Download the result as a transparent PNG.

Everything runs locally in your browser for speed and privacy.

When to Use Each Mode

  • Color Picker: Best for solid or evenly colored backgrounds (studio white, green screen, sky).
  • Lasso: Best for complex, multi-color backgrounds or subjects with similar background colors.

Pro Tips

  • Multiple clicks: Use Color Picker on several spots to capture different shades.
  • Feather edges: A small feather (10–25) blends hair and soft edges naturally.
  • Side-by-Side preview: Enable it for precise before/after checks.
  • Lighting matters: Even lighting and high-contrast subjects produce the cleanest cutouts.
  • Export PNG: Keep transparency for product pages, collages, and layered designs.

Troubleshooting & FAQs

Edge looks jagged? Increase feather slightly and try a lower tolerance for finer control.

Subject partly erased? Click more background shades (Color Picker) or switch to Lasso for tricky areas.

Large images feel slow? Close heavy tabs and keep this tab in focus to free CPU/RAM.

Are my files uploaded? No—everything happens in your browser.
