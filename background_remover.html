🎨 Why the Paint Roller?

A couple at a pop-up market needed to swap T-shirt colors for mockups fast. They didn’t want Photoshop—just “paint this blue to red with the highlights intact.” That sparked this Paint Roller tool: click to pick the source color, set tolerance, and brush on the new shade.

How to recolor

  1. Alt+Click on the image to pick the source color (or use Pick Source)
  2. Choose your target color and set tolerance
  3. Start Painting and brush over the area
  4. Use Preserve brightness to keep natural highlights/shadows

Everything runs locally in your browser. Images never leave your device.
