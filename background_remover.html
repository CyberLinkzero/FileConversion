Why this editor?

I built this after hearing a photographer and a seller at a café struggle with quick edits: “I just need to erase the background and brighten the subject for my listing—without learning a big program.” That conversation became this page: a fast, private, no-install editor that runs entirely in your browser.

What you can do here

  • Erase backgrounds with a soft or hard brush (zoom in for precision)
  • Crop, invert, grayscale, tweak brightness/contrast/saturation
  • Fix orientation with rotate/flip
  • Export high-quality PNG (transparent) or JPG

Privacy-first

All edits are local: your images never leave your device. It’s ideal for product photos, social posts, and classroom work.

Quick start

  1. Upload your image
  2. Pick a tool: Background, Erase, Adjust, or Crop
  3. Use Ctrl+Z to undo a step, Ctrl+Y to redo
  4. Download your result as PNG (transparent) or JPG
