Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2025

How the tool works

This site performs conversions locally in your browser. Files you select are processed on your device using in-browser libraries (e.g., Canvas, WebAssembly). Your files are not uploaded to our servers.

Information we collect

  • Operational data: Basic, anonymous usage events (e.g., which converter tab was opened) to improve UX. We do not collect your file contents.
  • Error logs: Aggregated diagnostics without personal file data.

Cookies & third-party services

We may use Google AdSense to serve ads. Google and other partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads and to personalize content.

  • Third parties (including Google) may serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites.
  • Learn how Google uses data from partner sites: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising: Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info/choices (NA), youradchoices.ca (CA), youronlinechoices.eu (EU).

Cookie categories we use

  • Essential — required for site functionality (e.g., remembering simple preferences).
  • Analytics — anonymous usage metrics to improve speed, reliability, and UX.
  • Advertising — used by AdSense and partners to limit ad frequency, measure performance, and (if enabled) personalize ads.

You can clear or block cookies through your browser settings. Guidance is available from your browser vendor.

Do we upload files?

No. All conversions are client-side. If we add cloud features in the future, they will be opt-in and documented here before launch.

Data retention

We do not store your files. Optional analytics retain only aggregated metrics for performance and abuse prevention.

Your rights

You may request a summary of the minimal, non-identifying analytics records associated with your sessions or ask us to delete any identifiable records we hold. Since we do not store file contents, we typically hold only aggregated usage data.

Children

Our services are intended for individuals aged 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

“Do Not Track”

Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” (DNT) setting. There is no common industry standard for DNT signals, so our site does not currently respond to them. We continue to review evolving standards.

Security

Because conversions happen locally, sensitive file contents remain on your device. We use standard security practices to protect our website code and any minimal analytics we store.

International users

Our site is operated from the United States. If you access it from other regions with laws governing data collection and use, you consent to the processing of minimal analytics data in the U.S. as described in this policy.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Email support@fileconverter.run or visit our Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our practices or legal requirements. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
