Why this page exists

This PDF toolkit came from real conversations with teammates and customers who were stuck juggling multiple sites: one to turn dozens of images into a single PDF, another to split pages, and yet another to extract text. I built a single, private page that does it all — in your browser, without uploading files. You keep control; we just make it simple.

All conversions and previews run locally using modern web tech (Canvas/WebAssembly). No server uploads.

PDF export tips

  • Images → PDF quality: Use “Fit to image” for full-resolution pages; adjust margins for printers.
  • Transparent pages: When turning PDF pages into PNG, pick transparent background for overlays.
  • File size: DPI and image quality drive size; 150–200 DPI is a good print/web balance.
  • Tables to PDF: Preview first — adjust column order in your CSV/XLSX before exporting.

FAQs

Do my files get uploaded? No. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Why does my DOCX look different? Word features like floating shapes/advanced styles don’t map 1:1 to HTML/PDF.

Big PDFs feel slow. Close heavy tabs, reduce image DPI, or split the PDF into sections.

Extracted text is empty. The PDF pages might be images; use a local OCR tool, then try again.
