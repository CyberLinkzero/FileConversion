Why this page exists

This PDF toolkit came from real situations where people had to jump across multiple websites: one tool for merging, another for extracting pages, another for turning images into PDFs, and another for exporting tables. This page brings those jobs together in one place — locally in your browser, without uploading files.

All conversions and previews run locally using browser APIs, Canvas, and WebAssembly-based tools.

PDF export tips

  • Use “Fit to image” for full-resolution image pages.
  • Adjust margins for printers and binders.
  • Choose transparent PNG output for overlays and design work.

Best workflow

  • Preview before downloading when possible.
  • Use moderate DPI for a better size/quality balance.
  • Split large jobs into sections if your browser slows down.

FAQs

  • Files are not uploaded to a server.
  • DOCX layouts may differ from Word.
  • Scanned PDFs may need OCR before text extraction.
