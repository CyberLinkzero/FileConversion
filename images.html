Convert & Optimize Images — Privately, In Your Browser

This tool converts and resizes images on your device. No uploads, no waiting — just fast results for JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, HEIC/HEIF, TIFF, SVG, and more.

  • Keep transparency: PNG and WebP preserve alpha channels.
  • Batch friendly: Select multiple images, set quality/size once, convert all.
  • Creator-ready: Export the right format for web, social, print, and e-commerce.

How to Use

  1. Click Select Files (or drag-and-drop images if your browser prompts).
  2. Choose an output format (PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF) and set Quality, Max width, and Max height.
  3. Optional: For HEIC/HEIF, your browser may need a decoder. We can add a polyfill on request.
  4. Press Convert Selected, then download from Results.

If your browser can’t encode AVIF/WebP, we automatically fall back (usually to PNG or JPEG).

Pro Tips

  • Right-size: Set Max width/height to your largest display size for faster pages.
  • Preserve transparency: Use PNG/WebP for product cutouts and UI.
  • Smart quality: Start JPEG/WebP around 80–85; raise only if needed.

Use-Case Playbooks

E-commerce: Remove background → export PNG/WebP with transparency → 1200×1200 product shots → compress.

Portfolios: Convert big PNGs to JPEG at quality 80 → cap width at 1600px.

Docs & blogs: Export charts as PNG (sharp text) → compress → insert into DOCX/HTML.

Troubleshooting & FAQs

Why did my animation stop? Conversions use the first frame of animated GIFs.

Images look dull? Ensure sRGB; wide-gamut sources may shift on web.

Max file size? Depends on device memory. Downscale via Max width/height for huge images.

Uploads? None. Everything runs locally in your browser.
