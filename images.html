Convert & Optimize Images — Privately, In Your Browser

This tool converts and resizes images on your device. No uploads, no waiting — just fast results for JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, HEIC/HEIF, TIFF, SVG, and more.

  • Keep transparency: PNG and WebP preserve alpha channels.
  • Batch friendly: Select multiple images, set quality/size once, convert all.
  • Creator-ready: Export the right format for web, social, print, and e-commerce.

How to Use

  1. Click Select Files (or drag-and-drop images).
  2. Choose an output format (PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF) and set Quality, Max width, and Max height.
  3. Optional: For TIFF, enable “Convert all pages.” For HEIC/HEIF, load the decoder when prompted.
  4. Press Convert Selected, then download from Results.

Everything runs locally in your browser for speed and privacy.

Choose the Best Format

Format Best For Pros Watch Outs
JPEG (JPG) Photos, social posts Small files at good quality; wide support Lossy; no transparency; avoid heavy recompression
PNG Logos, UI, graphics, screenshots Lossless; preserves transparency Larger files for photos
WebP Modern web images, mixed content High quality at small size; supports transparency Old browsers may lack support
AVIF Cutting-edge web performance Excellent compression and quality Browser support varies; slower to encode
GIF Simple animations, tiny icons Wide support; simple Large for photos; limited colors; first frame only when converting
TIFF Scans, multi-page docs High fidelity; can hold multiple pages Big files; convert to PNG/JPG/WebP for sharing
SVG Logos & icons (vector) Infinite scaling; tiny files for line art Rasterize to PNG for social or thumbnails

Pro Tips for Best Results

  • Right-size first: Set Max width/height to the largest display size you need — smaller images load faster.
  • Use sRGB: For web, stick to sRGB for consistent colors across devices.
  • Preserve transparency: Choose PNG or WebP when you need transparent backgrounds.
  • Dial quality smartly: For JPEG/WebP, start around 80–85 quality; drop a bit if file size matters.
  • Batch workflows: Convert multiple images at once for consistent sizes and quality.
  • Large images: Close heavy tabs and keep this tab focused to give your browser more memory/CPU.

Troubleshooting & FAQs

HEIC/HEIF won’t open? Load the HEIC decoder when prompted, then re-select your files.

Why did my animation stop? Conversions use the first frame of animated GIFs. Export as MP4/WebP for animation workflows.

Images look dull after export? Ensure your source is sRGB; converting wide-gamut images to web may change appearance.

What’s the max file size? It depends on your device memory. For huge images, downscale using Max width/height.

Are my files uploaded? No. Everything runs locally in your browser.
