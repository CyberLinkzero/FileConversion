Field mapping tips

  • Latitude / Longitude: use WGS84 decimal degrees like 45.523 and -122.676
  • Elevation: meters above sea level, if available
  • Time: ISO UTC is best, but Auto also handles Excel serial and Unix time
  • Downsampling: reduce points before sharing very dense tracks
  • Privacy: trim points near home or private sites before publishing

What the Stats & Splits show

FAQs & Troubleshooting

My CSV won’t map. Make sure latitude and longitude columns are selected and numeric.

Times look wrong. Set the correct time format like ISO, Excel, Unix seconds, or Unix milliseconds.

Big files feel slow. Close heavy tabs and consider splitting or downsampling the route.

Do my files upload? No. Everything runs locally in your browser.
