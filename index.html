Our Story

We built FileConverter.run because we were tired of upload-to-convert websites that slow you down and compromise privacy. Our mission: give everyone a fast, private, in-browser toolbox that handles everyday file work securely.

How It Works

  1. Load a tool — the converter code loads into your browser.
  2. Convert locally — files are processed on your device, not uploaded.
  3. Download instantly — results never touch our servers.
