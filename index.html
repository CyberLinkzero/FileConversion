Our Story

We created FileConverter.run because most converter websites force you to upload your files to their servers. That can be slow, risky, and often ends with watermarks, limits, or surprise paywalls.

FileConverter.run is different. Our tools run completely on your device using secure in-browser processing. Whether you’re working with PDFs, images, audio, video, spreadsheets, or code files, your data always stays with you.

Our mission is simple: powerful tools, instant results, total privacy — free for everyone.

How It Works

  1. Open any tool – the app loads directly in your browser.
  2. Pick your file – conversion and editing happen locally on your device.
  3. Download instantly – your file is never sent to our servers.

This approach gives you:

  • ⚡ Fast performance powered by modern browser tech
  • 🛡 True privacy – no file uploads, no server storage
  • ✅ No accounts or logins required
  • 📶 Offline-friendly use once a tool is loaded

FAQ

Do my files ever leave my computer?
No. All conversions and edits happen directly in your browser. We never receive, store, or access your files.

Is FileConverter.run free?
Yes. Our tools are free for personal and commercial use. Privacy-respecting ads help keep the platform online.

Do you track or profile users?
No. We don’t require accounts, sign-ins, or file uploads. You can control optional analytics and ads via our cookie settings.

What file types do you support?
We support images like PNG, JPG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, SVG, BMP, and GIF; audio like MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC, FLAC, M4A, and Opus; video like MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, and AVI; documents like PDF and DOCX; data formats like CSV, TSV, JSON, XML, XLSX, GPX, KML, and TCX; plus ZIP workflows and more.

Will more tools be added?
Yes. We’re actively adding new converters, editors, and utilities — including advanced file repair tools and browser-based “cyber” utilities.
