Our Story

We were tired of upload-to-convert sites that make you wait, watermark your results, or quietly store your files. So we built a different kind of toolbox: one that lives entirely in your browser. With FileConverter.run, the app code loads once — then every conversion happens locally on your device.

It’s faster. It’s private. It’s simple. From students and creators to developers and small businesses, our mission is the same: let you work with files without giving them away.

How It Works (No Uploads)

  1. Load a tool — the page fetches a tiny web app and libraries.
  2. Process locally — your file is read in memory; conversion runs inside your browser (Web APIs/WebAssembly).
  3. Download instantly — results come straight from your device’s memory, never from a remote server.

Tip: For big videos, keep the tab in focus and close heavy background apps to free up RAM.
