CyberPets – Virtual Cyber Yard Pet Game

CyberPets is a cozy browser-based virtual pet game set in a cyber yard. Raise animals, keep them fed and watered, clear hazards, and watch their personalities and behaviors evolve as they level up. Everything runs locally in your browser – no downloads, no accounts, just play.
